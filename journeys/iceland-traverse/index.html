Iceland Highland Traverse

Twelve days crossing Iceland's volcanic interior — Laugavegur, Landmannalaugar, and the Westfjords coastline.

DAY-BY-DAY ITINERARY

01

Reykjavik Arrival

Evening briefing at the base. Kit inspection and cultural orientation. First taste of skyr and fermented shark.

02

Landmannalaugar

The obsidian and rhyolite highlands. Multicolored mountains — impossible greens and burnt oranges side by side.

03

Laugavegur Day 1 — Hrafntinnusker

Through fumaroles and snow fields. Steaming vents melt channels in the snowpack. Ice caves nearby.

04

Laugavegur Day 2 — Álftavatn

Descent to the alpine lake. Geese nesting. Wind from the east. The silence that only uninhabited land has.

05

Laugavegur Day 3 — Emstrur

Crossing the Bláfjallakvísl glacier river. Cold. Fast. Unforgettable. Lunar landscape of Emstrur.

06

Þórsmörk

Valley of the gods. Hidden from the rest of Iceland by three glaciers. Rare vegetation in a protected microclimate.

07

Rest Day / Vatnajökull

Drive to Europe's largest glacier. Ice caving in electric blue chambers. Glacier walking above the void.

08

East Fjords Drive

Dramatic coastal cliffs. Fishing villages. Puffin colonies at the cliff edge.

09

Snæfellsjökull

Jules Verne's gateway to the center of the earth. Crater hike on the volcanic summit.

10

Westfjords — Dynjandi

The cascade of waterfalls that few visitors reach. Seven tiers, widening as they fall.

11

Westfjords Coast

Remote peninsula hiking. Arctic fox territory. Sea cliffs above the Denmark Strait.

12

Return to Reykjavik

Debrief, photographs, farewells.
